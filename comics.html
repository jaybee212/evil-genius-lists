Comic-Book Villains: The List of Lists

Villains are ordered by mentions, the number of lists they appear on, so unranked lists count as fully as ranked ones. Ties are broken by rank score: each ranked list gives 1 point for first place, falling towards 0 for last, and an unranked list gives 0.5.
